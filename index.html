Privacy policy

Personal installation · Owner access

The work stays
on your desktop.

MomentVault Local prepares short video edits on the owner’s PC and connects the owner’s YouTube channel for publishing and statistics.

This website explains the application and its data practices. The desktop installation has no public registration.

Read the privacy policy ↗MomentVault channel ↗
01

Local production

Transcription, visual analysis, captions and rendering run on the owner’s computer. Media is sent to YouTube only when the publishing workflow runs.

02

Owner-authorized access

Google authorization connects the owner’s channel. The application can upload videos, confirm channel details and retrieve authorized video statistics.

03

Visible data controls

The local application provides controls to delete measurements, reset its editing controller and disconnect YouTube. Revoking access remains available in the Google account.

About this installation

MomentVault Local is a personal creator tool in development. It is operated by its account holder for the MomentVault channel. Connecting an account does not start publishing or enable learning from statistics.
