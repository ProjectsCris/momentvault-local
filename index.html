The MomentVault
editing desk.

MomentVault Local is the desktop tool used to prepare short clips for the MomentVault channel.

Editing happens on the owner’s Windows PC. The app handles transcription, captions and rendering, then connects to YouTube for uploads and video statistics.

Visit the channel ↗Read the privacy policy

On the PC

Transcription, captions and rendering run on the PC. Codex performs editorial analysis over the internet using selected transcripts, frames and context. Completed media goes to YouTube when publishing is enabled.

This is a personal installation in development. There’s no public registration or download on this website.

Accounts & data

The owner authorizes access to a YouTube channel. The app can upload clips, check channel details and read authorized video statistics.

Connecting a channel does not start publishing or enable learning from statistics. The local app has controls to delete measurements and disconnect YouTube.
